School meals pilot: evaluation 2025

1 Summary

In 2023 forty primary schools began serving a free lunch every school day. This report evaluates what the pilot did for attendance and what it cost.

Attendance rose by 4.2 percentage points in the pilot schools, compared with matched schools that did not take part. The effect is largest for pupils in the lowest income quartile.

We find no effect on test scores in the first two years. Two years is a short time for an effect on learning to show.

2 Background

The pilot was decided in 2022 and covers schools in four municipalities, chosen among those that applied.
