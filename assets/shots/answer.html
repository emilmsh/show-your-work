What did the pilot achieve, and what did it cost?

The pilot raised attendance by 4.2 percentage points compared with matched schools that did not take part report, p. 1, and the effect is largest for pupils in the lowest income quartile. The estimate comes from a difference-in-differences model with school and year fixed effects analysis, line 14, with standard errors clustered at the school level appendix, p. 2.

There is no effect on test scores in the first two years report, p. 2.

Cost

The model puts the total at 9.5 million kroner a year model, B2, which is 4,800 kroner per pupil model, B3. The deck recommends two more years slides, slide 4.

One figure I could not confirm: the meeting notes do not say that all 170 schools reported ✗ notes. They say 160.
