# Meeting notes, 12 November

Present: the project group and the client.

## Status

Data collection is finished. We have attendance records for all 160 schools,
and the match between pilot and control schools holds up.

## Findings so far

- Attendance rose most among pupils from low-income families.
- The effect is largest in the first year and smaller in the second.
- Nothing suggests that single schools drive the result.

## Next steps

A draft of the report goes to the client by 5 December. The client wants a
separate chapter on uncertainty, with sensitivity analyses for the discount
rate.

## Open questions

Should the cost include the kitchens that were built for the pilot? The client
leans towards leaving them out, since they will be used after the pilot too.
